# Design decisions

Parents need to see what a teen can build and how the eight weeks work. The notebook makes each phase tangible: selecting Observe, Shape, Build, or Launch replaces the cover illustration, week range, and learning objective. The paper, ink and pencil palette continues through the student work, journal and editor. Native scrolling and short transitions keep the page usable without precise pointer gestures. The layout stacks the frame underneath the headline on phones.

The student index shows One Last Chapter, KrishiDrishti and Paws of Promise. Descriptions are paraphrased from Tactyqal’s own site. The consultation button leads to the same calendar embedded on the original site. Fees and testimonials were omitted rather than inventing or freezing temporary offers.

References reviewed: [Tactyqal](https://www.tactyqal.com/) for program facts, [PostHog](https://posthog.com/) for making the interface itself an explanation, [Loom](https://www.loom.com/) for teaching a product through a focused demonstration, and [Awwwards' design notes on Humana and other winners](https://assets.awwwards.com/awards/gallery/2023/07/HOT-RIGHT-NOW-BOOK-2023.pdf) for restrained interactions and legible editorial hierarchy. All notebook and project artwork was generated as original vector geometry for this implementation.
